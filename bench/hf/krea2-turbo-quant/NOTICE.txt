This repository distributes Derivatives of the Krea 2 Turbo model, as defined in that Agreement.
The Krea Model was MODIFIED: the 224 Linear layers of the Krea 2 Turbo transformer were re-encoded
from BF16 into ComfyUI's ConvRot `convrot_w4a4` format (4-bit weights, 4-bit activation path,
Hadamard rotation at group size 256) in `krea2_turbo_w4a4.safetensors`, and into a mixed 4-bit /
8-bit assignment (173 `convrot_w4a4` + 51 `asym_w4a8_int8`) in `krea2_turbo_mixed.safetensors`.
No weights were fine-tuned and no architecture was altered; every other tensor is preserved from
the source byte-for-byte.
