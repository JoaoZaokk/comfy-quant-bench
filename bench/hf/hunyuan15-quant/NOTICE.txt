STATEMENT OF MODIFICATIONS (Tencent Hunyuan Community License Agreement, s.3(b))
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The .safetensors files in this repository ARE MODIFIED FILES. They are not the
files Tencent published.

They are derived from `hunyuanvideo1.5_720p_t2v_fp16`, an FP16 single-file
repackaging of tencent/HunyuanVideo-1.5, by quantizing selected Linear weights
of the transformer to 4-bit or 8-bit integer formats native to ComfyUI
(`convrot_w4a4` and `asym_w4a8_int8`).

What was changed, per modified file:

  - Selected `double_blocks.N.*` Linear weights were replaced by an INT8
    container holding packed values plus a per-row FP32 scale tensor, and a
    per-layer quantization record was written into the safetensors
    `__metadata__`.
  - Which layers, and to which of the two formats, differs per file and is
    recorded in that file's accompanying `.quant.json` sidecar.
  - Every tensor NOT selected for quantization is preserved byte for byte from
    the source and was verified as byte-identical.
  - No weights were retrained, fine-tuned, merged, pruned or otherwise altered
    in value beyond the quantization described above.

Three of these files produce degraded or unusable output. That is deliberate and
is stated in the model card: they are published as measured negative results,
not as usable checkpoints.
